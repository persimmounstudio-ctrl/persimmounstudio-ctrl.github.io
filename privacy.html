Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Persimmon Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our mobile apps and games (the "Apps"), including:

  • River Pilot — package name com.persimmon.riverpilot
  • Luma Frame — package name com.persimmon.lumaframe

In short: we don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email or contacts inside our Apps. Photos and videos you take stay on your phone. Apps that show ads use Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to serve those ads.

1. Information we collect ourselves

Our Apps do not require registration, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number or email address.

Settings and progress (for example high scores, light mode or sound preferences) are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this only to reply to you.

2. Camera, microphone and your media (Luma Frame)

Luma Frame asks for permission to use your camera and, for video recording, your microphone. These are used only to show the camera preview and to take the photos and videos you choose to capture.

  • Photos and videos are processed on your device and saved to your phone's storage or gallery.
  • We do not upload, view, store or share your photos, videos or audio.
  • You can withdraw camera or microphone permission at any time in your Android settings.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Some of our Apps (currently River Pilot) show ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped)
  • Diagnostic information related to ad delivery

This information is used to deliver ads, limit repeats, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show personalised ads. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Consent (UK & EEA users)

If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tools. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalised ads based on context rather than your personal data. You can change your choice from the privacy option inside the App, where available.

5. Controlling ads on your device

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

6. Google Play

Our Apps are distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about your download and use of the Apps under its own terms and privacy policy.

7. Children

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the third-party services described above (Google AdMob and Google Play), we do not share information with others.

9. Retention and security

We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to reply and for our records. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies. We take reasonable steps to protect any information we hold, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, email us at the address below. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
